The Executive Summary
This report cross-references scattered public documents to expose the systemic financial and administrative collapse of Thabazimbi Local Municipality.

The municipality is not failing it has failed. The data reveals:

Deficit Explosion: The deficit grew by 268% in one year (from -R57.7 million to -R212.7 million).

Collection Crisis: The municipality is operating on only a 25.3% collection rate. Mines in the area paid only 0.8% of what they were billed.

Administrative Hollowing: 83.3% of senior management positions are vacant, including the Chief Financial Officer and the Office of the Municipal Manager.

Infrastructure Betrayal: Of the R20.3M received for the MIG infrastructure grant, R14.8M was left unspent, while 0km of the promised 4km of water pipelines were built.

Why This Matters
The narrative in South Africa is often that municipalities fail because of a lack of funding. This dashboard proves that Thabazimbi is a case study in administrative failure and enforcement collapse, not just a lack of revenue. You cannot deliver services with an empty administration.
